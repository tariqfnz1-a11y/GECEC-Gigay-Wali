Who We Are

Opening doors through education

GECEC is a non-profit, free community service initiative created to provide the children and parents of Gigay Wali with educational guidance, career counseling, modern skills training and future planning.

Its purpose is to bridge the gap between rural students and educational, technological, professional and global opportunities.

“To provide every child of Gigay Wali with quality education, proper guidance, modern skills and equal opportunities.”

Our vision is to help students demonstrate their potential in Pakistan and around the world.

Student Selection

Finding potential and commitment

The initial phase will serve a limited number of promising, hardworking and talented students.

Basic Criteria

  • Willingness to learn
  • Regular attendance
  • Parents’ cooperation
  • Teacher recommendation
  • Initial diagnostic assessment

Our Approach

Selection will consider more than academic marks. GECEC will look for commitment, potential, learning attitude and willingness to make consistent progress.
